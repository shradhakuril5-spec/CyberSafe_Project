▶ DECRYPT
🔒 AES-256-GCM:

This tool uses AES-GCM (Galois/Counter Mode) — the same encryption standard used by banks and governments. Your passphrase is converted into a 256-bit key using PBKDF2 with 100,000 iterations and a random salt. The IV (Initialization Vector) is randomized per encryption.
